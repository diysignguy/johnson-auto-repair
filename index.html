Before you call

Common Questions

Is an engine swap actually worth it?+

Very often, yes. If the body, transmission and frame are solid, replacing an engine usually costs far less than replacing the vehicle — and you keep a truck you already know. Call us with the year, make, model and what happened, and we’ll give you a realistic picture before you spend anything.

My A/C just needs a recharge, right?+

If it was full last summer and it’s low now, the refrigerant went somewhere — that means a leak. We test the system and find the leak first, so you’re not paying to fill it again every few months.

Do I need a Missouri safety inspection this year?+

Missouri exempts vehicles that are under 10 model years old and have fewer than 150,000 miles. Once your vehicle passes either mark, it needs a safety inspection every two years. Selling privately? The buyer needs a certificate less than 60 days old. Not sure where you land — just call.

Do you only do engines and A/C?+

No — we’re a full general repair shop. Brakes, transmissions and driveline, suspension and steering, cooling, electrical, exhaust, fuel systems, tune‑ups, diagnostics, diesel and routine maintenance all get done here. Engine swaps and A/C are simply what we’re best known for.

Do you need an appointment?+

Call ahead whenever you can. Bigger jobs like engine swaps get scheduled so the parts are here and the bay is open when your vehicle arrives. Inspections and quick checks we work in as the day allows.

What kinds of vehicles do you work on?+

Domestic and import cars, SUVs, and light‑ and heavy‑duty trucks, gas and diesel. If we’re not the right shop for your particular vehicle, we’ll say so instead of learning on your dime.
